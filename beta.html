Red Panic · closed beta

Beta kit

The closed beta designs levels with us: every place starts as a plan, and testers can redraw any of them and send it back.

Testers get the BETA ROM, the whole journey, with a guide, a question sheet and the plans with their redraw mode. Every redraw is read; none is promised a place in the game.

To join, send a private message on Discord to @henzelince.

The plans of the forty places

Eight planets, five places each, drawn on their depth planes with the idea each place is built on.

Reading a plan: three planes stacked, the front at the bottom (the brightest), the back at the top (the darkest). The thick dashes: the route of a player discovering the place; the thin dots: the way around each obstacle; the pilot stands at his start. Click a plan to see it larger.

VERDANT · Gravity 0, the gentlest. The hedge (a low wall of grass) and the stream. Paired pads: there and back to the same spot.

GLACIA · Gravity 1. The floor is ice: the pilot slides. The ice wall brakes; thin ice carries the pilot once, then opens into water (it forms again at each try). Offset pads: you land somewhere else.

ABYSSA · Gravity 2, under water. Slower spheres and line. Deep water stops the pilot; the ferry shuttles by itself. One-way pads.

AERIA · Gravity 2. The wind pushes the spheres AND the pilot to the left. The spheres sink into the cloud bed and bounce; the pilot cannot walk on it. Springs; the circuit: a one-way loop across three planes.

PRISMA · Gravity 3. The crystal wall: two lines break it, a shortcut never needed. The slabs, fixed blocks of the world.

NOX · Gravity 4. The shadow veil only shows in the glow of the spheres. The hidden pad: a discovery, never the only way.

FORGIA · Gravity 5. The belt carries the pilot, on the ledges too. The crane over the lava, the travelling pad, the metal walls.

MAGMA · Gravity 6, the heaviest. The lava, the rock bridge, the rafts on the lava, the rock walls, the geysers; every kind of pad.
