Red Panic · the level-design reference

Every place of the game starts as a plan. Here are the plans of the forty places of the eight planets, open to all, as the designers keep them. The pages are written in French; the guide is in French and English.

  • The plans: a tab per planet, its five places sketched on their depth planes, each with its question, its routes and its pressure; the ELEMENTS tab shows every element as the game draws it.
  • The guide: how to read a sketch, the doctrine in plain words, how to redraw a place.
  • The doctrine: what every place must do, and why.
  • The cards: each place in words, its question, its moment, its furniture and its route.

Redraw a place

In the plans, every place has a Redessiner (redraw) button: draw over the sketch, write what would be better, then Enregistrer (save) keeps a picture of it. Or print the plans and draw on paper.

Send it by private message on Discord to @henzelince. Every redraw is read; none is promised a place in the game.
